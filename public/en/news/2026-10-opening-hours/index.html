October opening schedule

In October, Saturdays and Sundays are lunch only. We are closed on public holidays, including Monday, October 12.

Regular weekday hours: 11:30–14:30 (last order 14:00) and 17:00–23:00 (last order 22:30). Saturdays and Sundays: 11:30–14:30 (last order 14:00).

On Thursday, October 22 (Buttoku Day), we are open continuously from 11:30 to 21:00, with last orders at 20:30. These differ from regular hours.
