Duck & Spring Onion Mazesoba continues in October

Our September special, Duck & Spring Onion Mazesoba, will remain available throughout October.

Duck and spring onion are seared to order, then paired with a rich egg yolk and our house sauce. Enjoy their aroma and the duck’s savoury flavour with our extra-thick noodles.

Available through October 31, from 1,580 yen including tax. Limited specials are not served on the 22nd (Buttoku Day).
